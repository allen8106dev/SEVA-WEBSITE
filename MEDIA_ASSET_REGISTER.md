## 3. Photo Identification & Leadership Governance Audit

### Policy Strictly Enforced:

> _"Do not infer someone's identity solely from visual resemblance... If identity cannot be reliably established from supplied filenames and organizational documentation, mark the portrait as requiring confirmation. Use the existing neutral portrait placeholder until confirmation is obtained. Do not publish incorrect identities."_

### Detailed Audit Findings:

1. **Rt Rev. Msgr. Dr. Shaji Mathews Vazhayil — Vicar General**:
   - **Supplied Asset**: `28 Msgr Shaji.png`
   - **Audit Verdict**: **Confirmed Official**. Filename and clerical status correspond directly to official leadership records. High-resolution studio portrait in white clerical cassock.
   - **Action**: Published as `/images/leadership/msgr-shaji-mathews-vazhayil.webp`.

2. **Very Rev. Dr. Joshy Vazhappillethu — Chancellor**:
   - **Supplied Asset**: `41 Fr Joshy.png`

   - **Action**: Published as `/images/leadership/fr-joshy-vazhappillethu.webp`.

3. **H E Dr. Mathews Mar Pachomios OIC — Eparchial Bishop**:
   - **Supplied Asset**: `ChatGPT Image Sep 8, 2026, 02_28_12 PM.png`

4. **Rev. Fr. Abraham Thanuvelil — SEVA Director**:

   - **Audit Verdict**: **Withheld — Identity Discrepancy**. The filename explicitly designates "Fr. Santhosh". SEVA's official directorate documentation confirms Rev. Fr. Abraham Thanuvelil as the registered Director.

## 6. Publication Safeguards & Child Protection

1. **Beneficiary Dignity**:
   All photographs depict beneficiaries with deep dignity, respect, and agency. Images capture acts of solidarity, care, educational empowerment, and nutritional support.
2. **Minors & Consent**:
   The supplied photographs were produced directly by SEVA Social Service Society for official reporting collages. When publishing images featuring children:
   - Private personal identities, school rosters, or individual names are never exposed in markup or alt text.
   - Descriptive alternative text focuses on the collective educational initiative (e.g., _"SEVA Vidya Jyothi school bag distribution to students in a community centre"_).
3. **Quarantine of Unconfirmed Originals**:
   Original archives and discrepancy portraits remain in `incoming-assets/`, strictly outside the public web root (`public/`). Large archives are excluded from Git commits via `.gitignore` to preserve repository hygiene.

## 7. How to Add New Media in Future Phases

1. Place raw incoming assets in a private folder outside `public/`.
2. Inspect metadata, subject, and authorization status.
3. For confirmed leadership portraits:
   - Crop to 4:5 ratio with head centered at approximately 30–35% from the top.
   - Export to `public/images/leadership/{normalized-name}.webp` at 800px width.
   - Update `src/content/leadership.ts` and `src/content/media.ts`.
4. For programme documentation:
   - Categorize by programme (Education, Healthcare, Women Empowerment, Community Welfare, Rural Development, Relief Work).
   - Export WebP derivatives (quality 85) without upscaling.
   - Register in `src/content/media.ts` with typed metadata and explicit `approvalStatus`.
   - Update relevant models in `src/content/focus-areas.ts`, `src/content/projects.ts`, or `src/content/home.ts`.
