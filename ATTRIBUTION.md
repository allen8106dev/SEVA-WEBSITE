## Replacement Instructions for Authentic SEVA Media

To replace these provisional images with authentic SEVA photographs:

1. Place the optimized web-format (WEBP or high-quality JPG) files in `public/images/`.
2. Update the `src` and descriptive `alt` text in `src/content/home.ts`.
3. Adjust the optional `focalPoint` property (e.g., `"50% 40%"`) to ensure optimal responsive cropping.
4. Update the visible `caption` to reflect authentic project context, photographer credit, and consent information.
